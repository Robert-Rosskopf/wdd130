My Favorite City

My favorite city is Los Angeles. I love hiking in the Santa Monica mountains, which run right through the center of Los Angeles. I was born in Santa Monica, and trails from the Santa Monica mountains run right down to the Pacific oceant. It is never too hot or too cold. I've walked down the beaches in the middle of winter, and although the water is a bit chilly, the walk is quite enjoyable. The ocean breeze is a natural air conditioner in the summer. And although one may get covered in sand, it is fun to swim in the ocean.
